How to find unpublished drafts in Confluence Cloud

You started a page last week, closed the tab, and now search cannot find it. That is normal: search only covers published content. Here are the three places where Confluence Cloud does show a draft, checked on a live site with a test draft.

Short answer. Open Recent in the left sidebar of the Confluence home. Unpublished pages are listed there with a Draft label. The same draft also sits in the space's content tree under the page you created it from, again with a Draft label, and on the Confluence home page in the block for picking up where you left off. Search and CQL will not return it.

Where the draft shows up

1. Recent

The Recent menu lists what you touched lately, newest first; a page that was never published carries a Draft label next to its title. At the bottom of the menu there is a link to all recent items, a full page with tabs that narrow the list to pages you worked on or own. There is no separate "drafts only" filter, so on a busy week the label is what you scan for.

2. The space content tree

In our test the draft appeared in the sidebar tree of its space, in the place where it will be published, with the same Draft label. If you remember roughly where you created the page, this is the quickest way back to it.

3. The home page

Confluence home has a block at the top for continuing recent work. A fresh draft showed up there first, above published pages.

The draft opens from these places through an address of the form /wiki/pages/resumedraft.action?draftId=…. Bookmark it if you will come back to a long draft over several days.

Why search does not find it

Confluence search, and CQL behind it, return published content. We created a draft with a unique word in its title, waited for the search index to catch up (about a minute, which is enough for published pages), and searched for that word: no result. CQL also has no condition for draft status; a query with status = draft is rejected as invalid. So "it must be somewhere, let me search harder" does not help. Use the places above instead.

What we could not find a way to do

Listing other people's unpublished drafts. We found no screen and no search query in Confluence Cloud for it, and Atlassian's Cloud documentation does not describe one; the pages about drafts in Atlassian's documentation that describe a Drafts list in the profile are written for Confluence Data Center. If a colleague has a page you need that was never published, the practical route is to ask them to publish it or share the draft with you.

If you know a way that works in Cloud, write to support@horizonventures.app and we will update this page.

Keeping drafts from piling up

Drafts do not show up in search, so they are easy to forget: the procedure someone started rewriting in March is still a draft in October, and the published version is still the old one. Two habits help. Publish early with a "work in progress" status on the page rather than keeping a private draft for weeks. And once a month, open Recent and either publish or delete every Draft you see.

The published side of the same problem, pages nobody has updated in a year, is easier to catch, because those pages are searchable. That is covered in how to find the Confluence pages nobody maintains and in the CQL cheat sheet.

Disclosure: I build Smart Search for Confluence, which shows live lists of published pages, including "not updated for N days". It uses the same search as Confluence, so it cannot list drafts either; for drafts, Recent is the answer.

Frequently asked

Where is my unpublished draft?

In Recent with a Draft label, in the space content tree under its parent page, and on the home page.

Why can't I find my draft with search?

Search only covers published content. A draft will not show up however long you wait.

Can an admin see other people's drafts?

We found no way to do it in Confluence Cloud. Ask the author to publish or share the draft.
