How to export multiple Confluence pages to Word

A handbook, a set of procedures for an auditor, a page tree someone wants "as one Word file". Confluence Cloud makes the single page easy and the many pages surprisingly hard. Here is what it does and does not do, checked on a live Confluence Cloud site in October 2026, and the routes that work.

Short answer. Confluence Cloud has no built-in way to put several pages into one Word document. The Word export on a page exports that page only, and the space export offers PDF, HTML, XML and CSV but not Word. The free route is to export the pages you need into one PDF (the space PDF export lets you pick them) and open that PDF in Word. If the document has to look right or is produced regularly, use an app that exports a page tree straight to Word.

What Confluence does natively

Word export: one page, no children

Every page has an Export to Word option in its actions menu; the same export sits at /wiki/exportword?pageId= followed by the page ID. We tested it on a parent page with two child pages: the file contained the parent page and nothing from either child. The file Confluence produces has a .doc extension but is a web page archive inside, which is worth knowing if your company's document templates or macros expect a real .docx; save it as .docx from Word before you pass it on.

Space export: no Word option

The space export page offers four formats on our site: PDF, HTML, XML and CSV. Word is not among them.

PDF export: you can choose the pages

The space PDF export gives two options: export everything, or choose what to export and tick the pages you want. The result is one PDF. This is the only native way we found to get a chosen set of pages into a single file.

The free route: one PDF, then Word

  1. Open the space PDF export and choose the pages you need.
  2. Download the PDF.
  3. Open the PDF in Microsoft Word with File, Open. Word converts it into an editable document.

Microsoft says the conversion works best with documents that are mostly text; complex layouts may not line up exactly. We did not test the Word side of this ourselves, so check tables and images in the result before you send it. For a document that is mostly headings, paragraphs and simple tables it is a reasonable free answer, and it costs about five minutes.

Two other free routes, both slower: export each page to Word and paste them into one document, which is fine for three pages and painful for thirty; or use the HTML space export and open the files in Word one by one.

Frequently asked

Can Confluence export several pages to one Word file?

Not natively. Export the pages to one PDF and open it in Word, or use an exporter app.

Does exporting a parent page to Word include its children?

No. Only the page you export ends up in the file.

Can I pick which pages go into a PDF export?

Yes, the space PDF export lets you choose the pages and gives you one PDF.
