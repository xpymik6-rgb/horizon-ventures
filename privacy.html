Privacy

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no advertising trackers, and asks you for nothing. It loads a webfont from Google Fonts, which means Google sees the request your browser makes for that file.

The apps

Clickable SVG and Smart Search run entirely inside Atlassian infrastructure under the Runs on Atlassian programme. We operate no servers of our own, so your pages, diagrams and attachments are never sent to us and never stored by us.

Each app publishes its own privacy statement and terms as part of its Marketplace listing:

  • Clickable SVG, privacy statement
  • Clickable SVG, terms of use
  • Smart Search, privacy statement

If you write to us

Support is handled over email. Whatever you send in that email, your address included, is kept in the mailbox and used only to answer you. It is not passed to anyone else and not used for marketing.

Billing

Payment for the apps is handled by Atlassian, not by us. We never see your card details. As the vendor we see the customer name, licence size and country that Atlassian reports to us.
